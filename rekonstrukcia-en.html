RENOVATION

In recent years, thanks to the intensive efforts of the leadership of the Prešov Jewish Religious Community and with the help of resources from the Central Union of Jewish Religious Communities, we renovated the façade and roof of the Orthodox synagogue, which houses the Jewish culture exhibition of the Museum of Judaica.

We successfully renovated the Klaus Sephardic Synagogue, a significant building in Prešov's Jewish quarter. We restored the building's roof and façade.
